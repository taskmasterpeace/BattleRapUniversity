## Introduction to Attributes

### What Are Attributes?

Attributes are the DNA of your battler. They represent your skill level across different aspects of battle rap - from your pen game to your stage presence to your mental toughness. Every battle you perform in, every prep session you complete, and every decision you make is filtered through these numbers.

**Think of attributes like this**: If battle rap was basketball, Lyricism is your shooting skill, Stage Presence is your vertical leap, and Resilience is your clutch gene. You don't need to be a 10 in everything to win, but you need to know what you're good at and how to leverage it.

### How Attributes Affect Battles

When you step on that stage, the simulation engine uses your attributes to calculate:
- **Segment scores** (30-second chunks of your round)
- **Peak moments** (haymakers that get the crowd going crazy)
- **Consistency** (can you maintain quality across 3 rounds?)
- **Crowd reaction** (are they feeling you or looking at their phones?)
- **Choke probability** (will you forget your bars under pressure?)

Higher attributes mean better performance, but the game isn't linear. A 9 in Wordplay with a 5 in Stage Presence plays VERY differently than an 8 in both. The system rewards specialization and punishes being mid at everything.

### How Attributes Improve Over Time

Your battler grows through experience:
- **Performance-based gains**: Battle well, your skills increase (+0.05-0.07 per attribute per battle)
- **Winner bonus**: W's accelerate growth (+0.02 extra to all attributes)
- **Loser penalty**: L's slow you down (50% reduced gains)
- **Prep impact**: More prep = 25% better improvement rate
- **Max growth cap**: 0.30 total points per battle (prevents overnight transformations)

You're not going from a 5 to a 10 in one battle. You're building a career. Like Lux didn't become Lux overnight - it took battles, evolution, and constantly sharpening the tools.

## Understanding the 1-10 Scale

### The Tier System

Battle Rap University uses a 1-10 scale broken into four tiers. These aren't arbitrary numbers - they represent real skill gaps in the culture.

#### **Low Tier (1-3): Rookie/Amateur Level**
- **What it looks like**: Basic bars, simple punchlines, struggling with delivery
- **Battle impact**: Getting outclassed regularly, high choke risk
- **Real equivalent**: Local battlers grinding their first 5-10 battles
- **Gameplay**: Avoid accepting battles against Mid-tier or higher until you improve

**Example Low-Tier Battler**:
- Lyricism: 3
- Wordplay: 2
- Stage Presence: 3
- Resilience: 2

You're going to get bodied A LOT. Focus on prep, take easier battles, build your way up.

#### **Mid Tier (4-6): Solid Circuit Battler**
- **What it looks like**: Competent writing, decent stage presence, can hang with most battlers
- **Battle impact**: Win some, lose some - competitive in your league
- **Real equivalent**: URL battlers on the undercard, respected regional names
- **Gameplay**: The sweet spot for balanced competition

**Example Mid-Tier Battler**:
- Lyricism: 6
- Wordplay: 5
- Stage Presence: 5
- Resilience: 6

You're a working battler. Not headlining Summer Madness, but you're getting booked and building a rep.

#### **Top Tier (7-9): Elite/Championship Level**
- **What it looks like**: Advanced writing, commanding presence, elite moments
- **Battle impact**: Favorite in most matchups, capable of classic performances
- **Real equivalent**: URL main stage regulars, tournament contenders

**Example Top-Tier Battler**:
- Lyricism: 8
- Wordplay: 7
- Stage Presence: 9
- Resilience: 8

You're THAT GUY. People watch your battles. You're in conversations. One or two attributes away from greatness.

#### **God Tier (10): GOAT Status**
- **What it looks like**: Transcendent skill, redefines what's possible
- **Battle impact**: You're the final boss
- **Real equivalent**: Prime Loaded Lux (Lyricism 10), Prime Hitman Holla (Stage Presence 10), Prime Rum Nitty (Wordplay 10)

**Example God-Tier Specialist**:

You're elite at your craft. Battles are winnable against anyone. You're chasing legacy.

### The Tier Break Points Matter

The difference between a 6.9 and a 7.0 is MASSIVE. Crossing into Top Tier (7+) changes how the league sees you:
- Better battle offers

## Writing Attributes Deep Dive

Writing attributes control your pen game - the bars you bring, the wordplay you weave, the creativity you display. In Small Room Circuit (writing-heavy league), these are make-or-break. In Main Stage Arena, they're still critical but not everything.

### Lyricism (1-10)

#### What It Does
Lyricism measures the DEPTH of your bars. Metaphor complexity, multi-layered meanings, poetic structure, quotable material. High Lyricism means you're writing bars that dissect on rewatch, that blogs break down line-by-line, that make people say "TALK TO EM."

#### Battle Impact
- **Small Room Circuit**: 1.8x weight (CRITICAL - this is your primary weapon)
- **Main Stage Arena**: 1.0x weight (still matters but balanced)
- Boosts segment scores through league-specific weighting
- Contributes to average score calculation
- Influences judges/crowd perception of "quality"

#### How to Improve
- **Writing prep focus**: Each day of writing prep adds +0.05 to Lyricism (base)
- **High average scores**: Score 7.0+ average across rounds → +0.05 gain after battle
- **Winner bonus**: Win the battle → +0.02 additional
- **Max gain**: ~0.07-0.09 per battle (with good performance + win)

#### Progression Math Example
You have Lyricism 6.5, you prep 5 days writing, battle and score 7.8 average, and win 2-1:
- High score trigger: +0.05
- Winner bonus: +0.02
- **Total gain: +0.07 → New Lyricism: 6.57**

After ~7-8 battles like this, you hit Lyricism 7.0 (Top Tier).

#### Real Battle Rap Equivalent
**Loaded Lux** is the prototype Lyricism 10:
- "You ain't die for your block, you just died ON your block" (layered meanings)
- Schemes that take 16 bars to set up
- Bars that age like fine wine

**Other high-Lyricism battlers**: Rum Nitty, JC, Tay Roc (technical pen), Illmaculate

#### Strategy Tips
- **Pair with Creativity (7+)** for devastating Small Room performances
- **Don't neglect Delivery** - complex bars fumbled = wasted lyricism
- **If Lyricism < 5**: Avoid Small Room Circuit until you improve
- **If Lyricism 8+**: You're a Small Room demon - hunt battles there

### Wordplay (1-10)

#### What It Does
Wordplay is your punchline power. Double entendres, flips, setups, "name" flips, homophones, sound-alike bars. This is the attribute that makes crowds go "OHHHHHHH." High Wordplay = high peak potential.

#### Battle Impact
- **Small Room Circuit**: 1.5x weight (highly valued)
- **Main Stage Arena**: 1.0x weight (still important)
- **CRITICAL**: Directly affects PEAK SCORE (haymaker moments)
- Wordplay 8+ with Haymaker threshold (8.5 peak) → +0.1 bonus to Creativity/Wordplay after battle
- Contributes to crowd reaction (people love clever flips)

#### Haymaker System Explained
Every segment has a 15% chance of being a "peak" (haymaker moment). If it triggers:
- Segment score gets 1.2x-1.4x multiplier (depending on prep)
- Crowd reaction gets +15 bonus
- If that peak score hits 8.5+, you get progression bonus after battle

**Wordplay is the attribute that enables haymakers to land.**

#### How to Improve
- **Writing prep focus**: +0.05 per writing day
- **Haymaker trigger**: Peak score 8.5+ → +0.10 to Wordplay (HUGE)
- **Winner bonus**: +0.02
- **Max gain**: ~0.12-0.15 per battle (with haymaker + win)

#### Progression Example
Wordplay 7.0, you prep 5 writing days, hit a 9.2 peak score, win 2-1:
- Writing performance: +0.05
- Haymaker bonus: +0.10
- Winner bonus: +0.02
- **Total: +0.17 → New Wordplay: 7.17**

Haymakers accelerate Wordplay growth FAST.

#### Real Battle Rap Equivalent
**Conceited** is Wordplay 10:
- "You sweet as Mike and Ike's, that's TWO FRUITS in ONE PACK"
- Every line flips, every bar has layers
- Crowd reaction on every punchline

**Other Wordplay masters**: Charron, Illmaculate, Tsu Surf, Danny Myers

#### Strategy Tips
- **High Wordplay + High Creativity** = Punchline King build (see Build section)
- **Wordplay 9+ with low Delivery** = bars going over people's heads (fix Delivery)
- **Pair with Stage Presence** to ensure haymakers get crowd reaction
- **Research prep** increases haymaker chance (angles create setups)

## Performance Attributes Deep Dive

Performance attributes control HOW you present your bars. You can have the best writing in the world, but if you can't deliver it with conviction, energy, and command, you're leaving points on the table. In Main Stage Arena (performance-heavy league), these are your bread and butter.

### Stage Presence (1-10)

#### What It Does
Stage Presence is your AURA. It's how you own the space, project confidence, command attention. When you step in the ring, does the room feel you? Or do you look nervous, small, out of place?

High Stage Presence means:
- You walk in like you belong
- Your posture, movement, eye contact all scream confidence
- The camera loves you
- Even your weaker bars land because of HOW you sell them

#### Battle Impact
- **Small Room Circuit**: 0.7x weight (less important in intimate settings)
- **Main Stage Arena**: 1.5x weight (CRITICAL - this is everything on big stages)
- **Crowd Reaction Multiplier**: Stage Presence directly boosts crowd reaction calculation
- **Momentum**: High Stage Presence + winning Round 1 = psychological advantage in Rounds 2-3
- **Judges**: Main Stage judges value performance heavily

#### Formula Deep Dive
Crowd reaction formula:
```
crowd_reaction = (segment_score / 10) × 60 + (performance_power / 10) × 40 × league.base_crowd_factor
```

Performance power = (Stage Presence + Crowd Control + Delivery) / 3

**Main Stage base_crowd_factor: 1.3** (30% boost to performance impact on crowd)

If you have Stage Presence 9, Crowd Control 8, Delivery 9:
- Performance power = (9 + 8 + 9) / 3 = 8.67
- Crowd component = (8.67 / 10) × 40 × 1.3 = **45.1**

That's MASSIVE. You're getting nearly half your crowd score from pure presence.

#### How to Improve
- **Performance prep**: +0.05 per day
- **High crowd reaction**: 75+ crowd average → +0.05 after battle
- **Winner bonus**: +0.02
- **Max gain**: ~0.07-0.09 per battle

#### Real Battle Rap Equivalent
**Hitman Holla** is Stage Presence 10:
- Walks in like he owns the building
- Every gesture, every step, every look is calculated dominance
- Could say a nursery rhyme and make it feel threatening

**Other Stage Presence GOATs**: Tay Roc, Loaded Lux (different energy but COMMANDING), Calicoe, Surf

#### Strategy Tips
- **Stage Presence 9+ in Main Stage** = you're stealing rounds on performance alone
- **Stage Presence <5 in Main Stage** = get bodied by performers even if your pen is better
- **Pair with Crowd Control (8+)** for maximum impact
- **Don't overlook this if you're a writer** - even Small Room values it at 0.7x

### Crowd Control (1-10)

#### What It Does
Crowd Control is your ability to READ THE ROOM and TIME your delivery for maximum reaction. It's knowing when to pause, when to let a bar breathe, when to amp up energy, when to go quiet for tension.

High Crowd Control means:
- You know which bars will hit and which won't
- You adjust mid-round if the crowd isn't feeling something
- You create moments (the pause before the haymaker, the build-up)
- You control the energy of the room

#### Battle Impact
- Contributes to performance power calculation
- **Boosts crowd reaction** (formula above)
- **Reduces stumble impact** (recovery skill = (Delivery + Crowd Control) / 2)
- If recovery skill 8+, stumbles only cost 10% instead of 15%
- **Synergy with Wordplay**: Timing makes punchlines land harder

#### Recovery Skill Explained
When you stumble (forget a line, slight hesitation), the game checks:
```
recovery_skill = (Delivery + Crowd Control) / 2
```

If recovery_skill ≥ 8.0:
- Stumble penalty: 0.90x (10% reduction) instead of 0.85x (15% reduction)

**Translation**: High Crowd Control helps you RECOVER from mistakes gracefully.

#### How to Improve
- **Performance prep**: +0.05 per day
- **High crowd reaction**: 75+ average → +0.05
- **Winner bonus**: +0.02
- **Max gain**: ~0.07-0.09 per battle

#### Real Battle Rap Equivalent
**Charlie Clips** is Crowd Control 10:
- Knows EXACTLY when to drop the punchline
- Pauses for effect, builds anticipation
- If a bar doesn't land, he moves on seamlessly
- The room is in his hand the entire time

**Other masters**: Tsu Surf (timing is immaculate), Geechi Gotti, Hollow Da Don

#### Strategy Tips
- **Crowd Control 8+ with Wordplay 8+** = your haymakers ALWAYS land
- **Low Crowd Control (<5)** = good bars fall flat due to bad timing
- **Pair with Stage Presence** for full performance build
- **Main Stage battlers NEED this** at 7+ minimum

### Delivery (1-10)

#### What It Does
Delivery is your VOCAL PROJECTION, clarity, conviction, and flow (rhythm/cadence). It's how clearly your bars are heard, how much power is behind your voice, how smoothly your words flow together.

High Delivery means:
- Every word is crisp and clear
- Your voice carries authority and conviction
- Your rhythm is tight - no awkward pauses or stumbles
- Even complex bars are delivered cleanly

#### Battle Impact
- Contributes to performance power calculation
- **Reduces stumble probability**: Delivery 8+ = -0.4% stumble chance per point above 5
- **Influences recovery skill** (see Crowd Control section)
- **Makes complex writing land**: High Lyricism + Low Delivery = bars going over heads
- **Crowd reaction**: Delivery affects how bars are received

#### Stumble Reduction Formula
```
stumble_probability = BASE (5.0%) - (performance_prep × 0.08%) - (delivery_above_5 × 0.08%)
```

Example: Delivery 9 with 5 performance prep days:
- Base: 5.0%
- Prep reduction: -0.4% (5 days × 0.08%)
- Ability reduction: -0.32% (4 points above 5 × 0.08%)
- **Final stumble chance: 4.28%** (vs 5.0% base)

Not huge, but in a 3-round battle (18 segments), that's the difference between 0.9 stumbles vs 0.77 stumbles.

#### How to Improve
- **Performance prep**: +0.05 per day
- **High crowd reaction**: 75+ average → +0.05
- **No stumbles in battle**: Clean performance gives small bonus
- **Winner bonus**: +0.02
- **Max gain**: ~0.07-0.09 per battle

#### Real Battle Rap Equivalent
**Pat Stay** was Delivery 10:
- Every word hit like a punch
- Conviction and power in every syllable
- Could deliver the most complex schemes without tripping
- Voice carried across any venue

**Other delivery masters**: Arsonal, Geechi Gotti (conversational but clear), Tsu Surf

#### Strategy Tips
- **Delivery 8+ is REQUIRED for Technical Writer build** (complex bars need clean delivery)
- **Low Delivery (<5) ruins high Wordplay/Lyricism** - fix this first
- **Pair with Crowd Control for elite performance package**
- **Main Stage Arena values this at 1.0x** - don't sleep on it

## Personal Attributes Deep Dive

Personal attributes represent your LIFE outside the ring. Battle rap doesn't exist in a vacuum - your financial situation, your reputation in the culture, your family support system all affect how you perform. These are indirect but powerful modifiers.

### Financial Stability (1-10)

#### What It Does
Financial Stability represents your money situation. Can you afford to travel to battles? Pay your bills without stress? Focus on prep without worrying about rent? Or are you battling because you NEED the check?

High Financial Stability means:
- You can prep properly without financial stress
- You can turn down bad matchups without worrying about money
- You can invest in your craft (coaching, better gear, etc.)

Low Financial Stability means:
- You're battling out of desperation
- Financial pressure increases choke risk
- You might accept bad deals just to get paid

#### Battle Impact
**CRITICAL INTERACTION WITH PREP**:
```
if (financial_stability < 4 AND total_prep < 5):
  choke_probability += (4 - financial_stability) × 0.8% × (1.0 - total_prep / 10)
```

Translation: Low money + low prep = PANIC MODE.

**Example**:
- Financial Stability: 2
- Total prep: 2 days (writing + performance + research)
- Choke increase: (4 - 2) × 0.8% × (1.0 - 2/10) = 2 × 0.8% × 0.8 = **+1.28% choke chance**

That's ON TOP of base choke probability. With base 1.5% + financial pressure 1.28% = 2.78% per segment.

Over 18 segments in a 3-round battle, that's **40% chance to choke** somewhere in the battle.

**BUT**: If you prep properly (8+ days), financial pressure disappears. Prep overcomes poverty.

#### How Financial Stability Improves
- **Life prep focus**: Each life day adds +0.025 to Financial Stability
- **Winning battles**: Payouts accumulate (not directly tracked in attributes, but meta)
- **Life events**: Positive events (sponsorships, side hustles) boost this

Financial Stability is SLOW to improve. It's not a battle-to-battle grind - it's career management.

#### Real Battle Rap Equivalent
Think about battlers grinding on small leagues for $500 vs battlers on URL getting $10K-$20K.

**Low Financial Stability (2-3)**: Battling on small leagues, struggling to pay bills, can't afford to travel
**Mid Financial Stability (5-6)**: Making decent money, bills covered, some savings
**High Financial Stability (8-9)**: URL main stage money, sponsors, comfortable

#### Strategy Tips
- **If Financial Stability < 4**: PREP HEAVILY (8+ days minimum) to offset choke risk
- **If you're broke and can't prep**: You're cooked. Expect to choke ~40-50% of battles
- **Invest in Life prep early career** to build stable foundation
- **Don't overlook this** - it's the difference between Verb vs Lux (choke city) and a clean performance

## Resilience: The Mental Game

### Resilience (1-10)

Resilience is THE MOST IMPORTANT ATTRIBUTE for consistency. It controls your ability to handle pressure, avoid choking, and perform under stress. You can have 10s in every writing and performance attribute, but if your Resilience is a 3, you're going to choke 40-50% of your battles.

#### What It Does
Resilience is your MENTAL TOUGHNESS. It's your:
- Ability to memorize and recall bars under pressure
- Composure when the crowd is against you
- Clutch gene in close battles
- Resistance to nerves, anxiety, mental breakdowns

High Resilience means:
- You perform CONSISTENTLY (even in high-pressure situations)
- Low choke risk (~3-7% per battle)
- You trust your preparation
- Mentally unshakable

Low Resilience means:
- You're a CHOKER (45-50% choke rate per battle if Resilience <4)
- Inconsistent performances
- Nerves wreck you
- You're a risk even in favorable matchups

#### Battle Impact - The Choke Formula

This is the most complex and CRITICAL formula in the game:

```
BASE CHOKE PROBABILITY: 1.5% per segment

REDUCTIONS:
- Resilience above 5: -(resilience - 5) × 1.5% per segment
- Writing prep days: -prep_days × 0.8% per segment
- Family Bond buffer: +family_bond / 10 to effective resilience
- Rest prep days: +rest_days × 0.05 to resilience (temporary)

INCREASES:
- Stress (0-100): +(stress / 100) × 10% per segment
- Low financial + low prep: +(4 - financial) × 0.8% × (1.0 - total_prep / 10) per segment
- Badge effects: Known Choker badge adds +7.0% per segment

FLOOR: 0.7% per segment (minimum - even gods can choke)
CAP: 25% per segment (maximum - prevents absurd choke rates)
```

#### Example Calculations

## How Attributes Improve: Progression System

### The Progression Formula

After EVERY battle, the game checks your performance and awards attribute improvements. This is automatic - you don't choose what improves. It's based on HOW you performed.

#### Improvement Triggers

**Writing Attributes (Lyricism, Wordplay, Creativity)**:
- **Trigger**: Average score ≥ 7.0 across all rounds
- **Gain**: +0.05 to Lyricism, Wordplay, Creativity

**Example**: You score 7.2, 7.5, 6.8 across 3 rounds → average 7.17 → TRIGGER
- Lyricism +0.05
- Wordplay +0.05
- Creativity +0.05

**Performance Attributes (Stage Presence, Crowd Control, Delivery)**:
- **Trigger**: Crowd reaction ≥ 75 average across all rounds
- **Gain**: +0.05 to Stage Presence, Crowd Control, Delivery

**Example**: Crowd reactions 82, 78, 71 → average 77 → TRIGGER
- Stage Presence +0.05
- Crowd Control +0.05
- Delivery +0.05

**Resilience**:
- **Trigger**: No chokes in any round
- **Gain**: +0.05

**Haymaker Bonus**:
- **Trigger**: Peak score ≥ 8.5 in any segment
- **Gain**: +0.10 to Creativity, +0.05 to Wordplay

**Example**: Round 2, Segment 3, you hit a 9.1 peak → HAYMAKER
- Creativity +0.10
- Wordplay +0.05

**Winner Bonus**:
- **Trigger**: Win the battle (2-1 or 3-0)
- **Gain**: +0.02 to ALL attributes that improved this battle

**Loser Penalty**:
- **Trigger**: Lose the battle
- **Effect**: All gains are multiplied by 0.5 (50% reduction)

#### Maximum Gain Cap

You can't gain more than **0.30 total attribute points per battle**. This prevents overnight transformations.

**Example calculation**:
- Writing trigger: +0.05 × 3 = 0.15
- Performance trigger: +0.05 × 3 = 0.15
- Resilience: +0.05
- Haymaker: +0.10 Creativity, +0.05 Wordplay
- Winner bonus: +0.02 × all
- **Total potential: 0.15 + 0.15 + 0.05 + 0.15 + 0.02×7 = 0.64**

Capped at 0.30, distributed proportionally:
- Scale factor: 0.30 / 0.64 = 0.47
- All gains multiplied by 0.47

Final gains:
- Lyricism: 0.05 × 0.47 = 0.024
- Wordplay: (0.05 + 0.05) × 0.47 = 0.047
- Creativity: (0.05 + 0.10) × 0.47 = 0.071
- Stage Presence: 0.05 × 0.47 = 0.024
- Crowd Control: 0.05 × 0.47 = 0.024
- Delivery: 0.05 × 0.47 = 0.024
- Resilience: 0.05 × 0.47 = 0.024

**Total: 0.238** (under cap, distributed efficiently)

### Prep Impact on Progression

**CRITICAL MECHANIC**: The more you prep, the MORE you improve after battles.

```
effective_gain = base_gain × (1 + preparation / 20)
```

**Example**:
- Base gain: +0.05 Lyricism
- Preparation attribute: 8
- Effective gain: 0.05 × (1 + 8/20) = 0.05 × 1.4 = **0.07**

That's 40% more improvement just from higher Preparation stat.

**Prep pattern bonuses** (from badges):
- **Technical Writer**: +35% writing prep efficiency → 0.05 × 1.35 = 0.0675 gain
- **Scheme Specialist**: +30% writing prep efficiency → 0.05 × 1.3 = 0.065 gain

## League Weight Differences

The game has two leagues with drastically different scoring systems. Understanding these differences is CRITICAL for build optimization.

### Small Room Circuit (2-Minute Rounds)

**Philosophy**: Intimate setting, focus on WRITING. The crowd is close, they're listening to every word, they appreciate technical skill and lyricism.

**Round structure**:
- 2-minute rounds
- 4 segments per round (30 seconds each)
- 12 total segments per battle

**Attribute weights** (how attributes contribute to segment scores):
- **Lyricism: 1.8x** (HUGE - your pen is everything)
- **Wordplay: 1.5x** (punchlines matter)
- **Creativity: 1.0x** (standard)
- **Stage Presence: 0.7x** (REDUCED - less important in small rooms)
- **Crowd Control: 1.0x** (standard)
- **Delivery: 1.0x** (standard)

**Base crowd factor**: 1.0 (standard crowd reaction)

**Optimal build for Small Room**:
- **MUST HAVE**: Lyricism 7+, Wordplay 7+
- **HELPFUL**: Creativity 7+, Delivery 7+ (to execute complex bars)
- **LESS CRITICAL**: Stage Presence (can be 5-6)

**Example segment score calculation**:
- Lyricism: 8 × 1.8 = 14.4
- Wordplay: 7 × 1.5 = 10.5
- Creativity: 6 × 1.0 = 6.0
- Stage Presence: 5 × 0.7 = 3.5
- Crowd Control: 6 × 1.0 = 6.0
- Delivery: 7 × 1.0 = 7.0
- **Raw power: 47.4**

Segment score formula:
```
base_score = (writing_power × writing_weight) + (performance_power × performance_weight)
```

Small Room has **higher writing weight**, so writing attributes dominate.

**Who thrives in Small Room**:
- Technical Writers
- Scheme Specialists
- Lyricism-focused builds
- Battlers with 8+ Lyricism/Wordplay

**Who struggles**:
- Pure performers (Stage Presence 10, Lyricism 5)
- Freestylers (inconsistent in structured 2-min format)
- Low writing attributes (<6)

### Main Stage Arena (3-Minute Rounds)

**Philosophy**: Big stage, big crowd, focus on PERFORMANCE. The crowd is far, they want energy, moments, crowd interaction. Writing still matters, but HOW you present it is critical.

**Round structure**:
- 3-minute rounds
- 6 segments per round (30 seconds each)
- 18 total segments per battle

**Attribute weights**:
- **Lyricism: 1.0x** (standard)
- **Wordplay: 1.0x** (standard)
- **Creativity: 1.0x** (standard)
- **Stage Presence: 1.5x** (HUGE - own the stage)
- **Crowd Control: 1.3x** (important - work the crowd)
- **Delivery: 1.0x** (standard)

**Base crowd factor**: 1.3 (30% boost to performance's impact on crowd reaction)

**Optimal build for Main Stage**:
- **MUST HAVE**: Stage Presence 7+, Crowd Control 7+
- **HELPFUL**: Delivery 7+, Wordplay 7+ (haymakers land better with crowd)
- **LESS CRITICAL**: Lyricism (can be 6-7 instead of 8+)

**Example segment score calculation** (same attributes as Small Room example):
- Lyricism: 8 × 1.0 = 8.0
- Wordplay: 7 × 1.0 = 7.0
- Creativity: 6 × 1.0 = 6.0
- Stage Presence: 5 × 1.5 = 7.5
- Crowd Control: 6 × 1.3 = 7.8
- Delivery: 7 × 1.0 = 7.0
- **Raw power: 43.3**

Lower than Small Room (47.4) despite same attributes, because this build is writing-focused.

**BUT**: Crowd reaction formula gives Main Stage 30% boost to performance impact:
```
crowd = (segment_score / 10) × 60 + (performance_power / 10) × 40 × 1.3
```

So performance builds get MORE crowd love on Main Stage.

**Who thrives on Main Stage**:
- Performers (Stage Presence 9+)
- Crowd Control specialists
- Balanced builds (7s across the board)
- Battlers with ENERGY and PRESENCE

**Who struggles**:
- Pure technical writers (Lyricism 10, Stage Presence 5)
- Introverted/low-energy battlers
- Small Room specialists trying to transition

### Strategic League Selection

**If your build is**:
- Lyricism 9, Wordplay 8, Stage Presence 5 → **STAY IN SMALL ROOM**
- Stage Presence 9, Crowd Control 8, Lyricism 6 → **GO TO MAIN STAGE**
- All 7s → **YOU CAN COMPETE IN BOTH** (versatile build)

**Career path considerations**:
- **Small Room pays less but easier competition** (good for building record)
- **Main Stage pays more but tougher competition** (good for reputation)
- **Switching leagues is HARD** - attribute weights punish mismatches

**Example**: Lyricism 9, Stage Presence 5 battler moves from Small Room to Main Stage
- Small Room performance: Lyricism 9 × 1.8 = 16.2 contribution
- Main Stage performance: Lyricism 9 × 1.0 = 9.0 contribution
- **You just lost 7.2 points of power** by switching leagues

Meanwhile Stage Presence 5 × 1.5 = 7.5 on Main Stage vs 5 × 0.7 = 3.5 on Small Room (+4.0 gain).

Net change: -7.2 + 4.0 = **-3.2 power loss**. You're getting bodied.

**DON'T SWITCH LEAGUES unless your build supports it.**

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Spreading Too Thin

**The Problem**: New players often distribute attributes evenly (all 5s or all 6s) thinking "well-rounded is safe."

**Why it's bad**: Battle Rap University REWARDS SPECIALIZATION. The league weighting system and badge mechanics favor battlers with elite strengths, not battlers with no weaknesses.

**Example**:
- **Battler A**: All attributes at 6.5 (Total: 45.5 points)
- **Battler B**: Lyricism 10, Wordplay 8, Delivery 8, Resilience 8, rest at 4 (Total: 46 points)

In Small Room:
- Battler A: 6.5 × 1.8 (Lyricism) + 6.5 × 1.5 (Wordplay) = **11.7 + 9.75 = 21.45 writing power**
- Battler B: 10 × 1.8 + 8 × 1.5 = **18.0 + 12.0 = 30.0 writing power**

**Battler B DOMINATES despite same total points.**

**The Fix**:
- Identify your PRIMARY attributes (2-3 attributes)
- Max those to 8-10
- Accept weaknesses in secondary attributes (4-6 is fine)
- Build around league weights

---

### Mistake 2: Ignoring Resilience

**The Problem**: Players focus on "sexy" attributes (Wordplay, Stage Presence) and neglect Resilience, thinking "I'll just prep well."

**Why it's bad**: Low Resilience (<5) creates a CHOKE CEILING. Even with perfect prep, you're at 7-10% choke risk per battle. That's 1 in 10-15 battles you just BLOW IT.

**Example career arc**:
- Battle 1: Resilience 3, perfect 10-day prep, score 8.0 average, WIN 2-1 → Great start!
- Battle 2: Resilience 3, perfect 10-day prep, CHOKE Round 2, LOSE 1-2 → Career stalled
- Battle 3: Resilience 3, 8-day prep, CHOKE Round 1, LOSE 0-3 → Reputation tanking
- **Result**: 1-2 record despite elite prep, media labels you "choker," badge earned

**The Fix**:
- **Resilience 7+ is MANDATORY for competitive play**
- If Resilience < 5 at creation, invest in:
  - Rest prep (every battle, 1-2 days minimum)
  - Life prep (build Family Bond to 7+ for +0.7 resilience buffer)
  - Clean performances (no-choke battles improve Resilience)
- **Target: Resilience 8+ by battle 15-20** (sustainable career path)

---

### Mistake 3: Wrong League for Build

**The Problem**: Players accept battles in whichever league offers them, not realizing league weights make or break performance.

**Why it's bad**: A Lyricism 10 battler in Main Stage Arena is getting 0% value from their highest attribute's league weight. Meanwhile Stage Presence 5 is actively hurting them.

**Example**:
- **Build**: Lyricism 10, Wordplay 9, Stage Presence 5
- **Small Room performance**: 10 × 1.8 + 9 × 1.5 + 5 × 0.7 = 18.0 + 13.5 + 3.5 = **35.0 power**
- **Main Stage performance**: 10 × 1.0 + 9 × 1.0 + 5 × 1.5 = 10.0 + 9.0 + 7.5 = **26.5 power**

**You lost 8.5 points of power** by choosing the wrong league.

Against an even matchup:
- Small Room: You're favored (35.0 vs ~30.0 opponent)
- Main Stage: You're underdog (26.5 vs ~30.0 opponent)

**The Fix**:
- **Know your build's league**:
  - Lyricism/Wordplay 8+ → Small Room
  - Stage Presence/Crowd Control 8+ → Main Stage
  - Balanced (all 7s) → Either league
- **Stay in your lane until you adapt** (improving Stage Presence takes 20+ battles)
- **Check battle offers for league** before accepting

### Mistake 4: Overprepping or Underprepping

**The Problem**: Players either max prep every battle (10 days) or minimal prep (2-3 days) without understanding diminishing returns or minimum thresholds.

**Why it's bad**:
- **Overprepping (10 days)**: Diminishing returns kick in, wasted time, stress accumulation
- **Underprepping (<5 days)**: Choke risk spikes, financial pressure kicks in, performance suffers

**Optimal prep curves**:
- **Resilience 8+**: 5-6 days prep is sufficient (0.7% floor choke anyway)
- **Resilience 5-7**: 7-8 days prep recommended
- **Resilience <5**: 8-10 days prep MANDATORY

**Example math (Resilience 6)**:
- 5 days prep: Choke 1.5% - (1 × 1.5%) - (5 × 0.8%) = floor at 0.7% → **~7% battle choke**
- 8 days prep: Same floor at 0.7% → **~7% battle choke** (NO DIFFERENCE)
- **3 extra days wasted**

But if Resilience 4:
- 5 days prep: 1.5% - 0% - 4.0% = floor at 0.7% → **~7%**
- 3 days prep: 1.5% - 0% - 2.4% = still floor → **~7%**
- **BUT financial pressure (+1.28%)** if Financial < 4 → **~17% choke**

**The Fix**:
- **Resilience 8+**: 5-6 days optimal (spend rest on life/other battles)
- **Resilience 5-7**: 7-8 days optimal
- **Resilience <5 or Financial <4**: 8-10 days REQUIRED
- **Don't overshoot** - use extra days for life prep (builds long-term stats)
