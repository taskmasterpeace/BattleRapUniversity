# Badge Category Reference Guide

Quick reference for all 94 badges organized by category, tier, and acquisition method.

---

## WRITING BADGES (26 total)

### Positive Writing (12)

#### Starter Options (5)
- **Wordplay Wizard** (Rare) - +40% wordplay, archetype option
- **Metaphor Master** (Rare) - +30% creativity, customization option
- **Multisyllabic Master** (Rare) - +25% lyricism +20% wordplay
- **Creativity Beast** (Rare) - +35% creativity, customization option
- **Rebuttal King/Queen** (Rare) - +20% creativity, improvisation

#### Earned Through Play (7)
- **Punchline King/Queen** (Legendary) - 10 haymaker moments (peak ≥ 8.5)
- **Scheme Specialist** (Legendary) - 15 battles with high lyricism
- **Technical Writer** (Legendary) - 20 battles with ≥ 8 prep days (archetype starter)
- **Angle Master** (Legendary) - 25 research-heavy battles (archetype starter)
- **Consistent Writer** (Legendary) - 10 battles with consistency ≥ 0.85
- **Pen Game Elite** (Legendary) - All writing attributes ≥ 9
- **Freestyle Genius** (Legendary) - 10 battles won with ≤ 3 prep days (archetype starter)

### Negative Writing (14)

#### Removable (7)
- **Recycler** - Low creativity pattern → Remove: 8 battles with creativity ≥ 7
- **Lazy Writer** - Consistently low prep → Remove: 10 battles with ≥ 6 prep days
- **Predictable Rhymer** - Limited creativity → Remove: 8 battles with varied content
- **Weak Punchline Setups** - Poor peak moments → Remove: 5 battles with peak ≥ 7.5
- **Shallow Research** - Poor research effectiveness → Remove: 10 research-heavy battles
- **Filler Abuser** - Padded time → Remove: 10 battles with high average score
- **Outdated Referencer** - Old references → Remove: Learn new content styles

#### Earned Through Failure (7)
- **Biter** - Caught stealing bars (scandal event)
- **Reach God/Goddess** - Poor wordplay despite trying
- **One-Trick Pony** - 15 consecutive battles, same content
- **Redundant** - Says same thing multiple ways
- **Overcomplicated** - Too complex, loses crowd
- **Cliche Abuser** - Overused metaphors
- **Name Flip Dependent** - Relies too heavily on name flips

## PERFORMANCE BADGES (20 total)

### Positive Performance (8)

#### Starter Options (4)
- **Charismatic** (Rare) - +35% crowd control, archetype option
- **Smooth Flow** (Rare) - +30% delivery +10% consistency
- **Aggressive** (Rare) - +25% delivery +20% stage presence
- **Theatrical** (Rare) - +30% stage presence, Main Stage bonus

#### Earned Through Play (4)
- **Crowd Favorite** (Rare) - Crowd reaction avg ≥ 80 for 10 battles
- **Unorthodox** (Rare) - Creative performance style
- **Speed Rapping** (Rare) - High delivery + fast pattern
- **Stage Domination** (Legendary) - All performance attributes ≥ 9

### Negative Performance (12)

#### Removable (4)
- **Choker** - 2 consecutive chokes → Remove: 5 clean battles
- **Underprepared** - Consistently low prep → Remove: 10 battles with ≥ 6 prep
- **Overprepared** - Ironically chokes from anxiety → Remove: 8 battles with balanced prep
- **Inconsistent Performer** - High variance → Remove: 5 consistent performances

#### Hard to Remove (8)
- **Mumbler** - Poor delivery, can't hear bars
- **Monotone Deliverer** - Lacks vocal variation
- **Poor Breath Control** - Stamina issues
- **Energy Drainer** - Kills crowd energy
- **Crowd Killer** - Negative crowd reaction pattern
- **Awkward Stage Presence** - Stiff, unnatural
- **Off-Beat Performer** - Timing issues
- **Stiff Body Language** - Lacks physical expression

## REPUTATION BADGES (28 total)

### Positive Reputation (9)

#### Earned Through Career Progression (9)
- **Respected Veteran** (Rare) - 20 battles + reputation ≥ 8
- **Resilient Battler** (Rare) - 10 battles without choking
- **Big Stage Performer** (Rare) - League-specific performance
- **Clutch Performer** (Rare) - 5 clutch comeback moments
- **Battle Technician** (Rare) - 15 battles with balanced prep
- **Consistent Grinder** (Rare) - 30 battles completed, high rate
- **Believable Persona** (Rare) - Authenticity + reputation
- **Battle of the Night Winner** (Rare) - 5 standout performances
- **Consummate Professional** (Legendary) - Reputation ≥ 9, 95% completion

### Negative Reputation (19)

#### Removable (6)
- **Known Choker** - 2 consecutive chokes → Remove: 5 clean battles
- **Drama Starter** - 3 drama choices → Remove: 5 drama-free choices
- **Sore Loser** - Poor sportsmanship → Remove: 3 graceful losses
- **Controversial** - Polarizing → Manage through professional choices
- **Clout Chaser** - Fame over substance → Build authentic reputation
- **Career Plateaued** - Stagnation → Improve 2+ attributes

#### Hard to Remove (7)
- **Unreliable** - 1 no-show or 2 cancellations → 20 consecutive completions to remove
- **Disrespectful** - 3 disrespectful choices → 15 respectful choices + apology
- **Backstabber** - Betrays allies → 25 battles + loyalty rebuilding
- **Bitter Veteran** - Resentful → Attitude shift over time
- **Culture Vulture** - Outsider → 30+ battles earning veteran respect
- **Living in Glory Days** - Stuck in past → Learn new content
- **Weak Chin** - Can't handle pressure → Hard to fix (attribute-based)

#### Permanent (6)
- **Fallen Star** - Past peak, declining (represents career arc)
- **Known Stealer** - Steals deposits (reputation destroyed)
- **Washed** - Past prime, all attributes -15%
- **Financial Struggles** - Life event or financial_stability < 3
- **Health Issues** - Life event trigger
- **Jail Risk** - Life event trigger
- **Substance Issues** - Life event trigger

## TOURNAMENT BADGES (3 total - Consolidated)

### All Earned Through Tournament Play

1. **Tournament Veteran** (Rare)
   - **Earned**: Complete 3+ tournaments
   - **Effects**: -3% choke, +5 crowd reaction, +8% consistency, +10% prize money

2. **Big Stage Specialist** (Rare)
   - **Earned**: Strong tournament performance pattern
   - **Effects**: +15% performance in tournaments, -5% in regular battles (trade-off)

3. **Cinderella Story** (Rare)
   - **Earned**: Reach finals as #13-16 seed
   - **Effects**: +20% peak segments, +8 crowd reaction, 30-day duration

**Removed Badges** (consolidated):
- Tournament Choker (use existing Known Choker)
- Tournament Grinder (merged into Tournament Veteran)
- Glass Cannon (use Punchline King + Inconsistent Performer)

## MULTI-TASKING BADGES (3 total - Phase 2)

### Implement When "Multiple Concurrent Battles" Feature Exists

1. **Multitasker** (Rare)
   - **Earned**: Successfully manage 3+ concurrent battles
   - **Effects**: +10% all prep, -20% stress from juggling

2. **Focused Specialist** (Rare)
   - **Earned**: Excel at single battles, struggle with multiple
   - **Effects**: +25% prep when focused, -20% when juggling

3. **Time Management Expert** (Legendary)
   - **Earned**: Successfully manage 5+ concurrent battles
   - **Effects**: +15% all prep, NO stress penalty (elite skill)

**Removed Badges**:
- Workaholic (overlaps with Consistent Grinder)
- Burnout Risk (punishing without interesting gameplay)

## BADGE PROGRESSION PATHS

### Evolution Arcs

**Choker → Clutch**:
1. Start: Known Choker (earned through failure)
2. Remove: 5 clean battles
3. Earn: Clutch Performer (5 clutch moments)

**Newcomer → Veteran → Legend**:
1. Start: No badges
2. Progress: Respected Veteran (20 battles, rep 8)
3. Ultimate: Consummate Professional (rep 9, 95% completion)

**Amateur → Specialist → Elite**:
1. Start: Wordplay Wizard (starter)
2. Progress: Scheme Specialist (15 high-lyricism battles)
3. Ultimate: Pen Game Elite (all writing ≥ 9)

**Comedy Evolution**:
1. Start: Comedy (starter badge)
2. Upgrade: Comedian (70%+ comedy usage)

**Storytelling Evolution**:
1. Start: Storytelling (starter badge)
2. Upgrade: Enhanced Storyteller (70%+ usage + high creativity)

**Controversy → Redemption**:
1. Start: Drama Starter OR Controversial (earned)
2. Redeem: Remove badges (5 drama-free choices)
3. Ultimate: Consummate Professional (full redemption)
