## What Are Badges?

Badges are your battler's identity markers. They tell your story: are you a punchline-heavy haymaker artist? A technical writer who grinds prep days? A controversial freestyler who wings it with minimal prep? Badges define who you are in the battle rap world.

**Key Points**:
- You start with **3 badges** at character creation (archetype + customization)
- You can earn **100+ badges** through gameplay
- Badges provide **mechanical bonuses** and **penalties**
- Negative badges can be earned through failure but most can be **removed through redemption**
- Your badge collection tells your career story

---

## Why Badges Matter

### Gameplay Effects
- **Prep Efficiency**: Technical Writer gets +35% writing prep effectiveness
- **Choke Rates**: Known Choker has +7% choke chance per segment (46% per battle)
- **Crowd Reactions**: Crowd Favorite gets +15 crowd reaction bonus
- **Peak Moments**: Punchline King/Queen gets +15% to peak segments (haymakers)
- **Consistency**: Freestyle Genius has wild variance, Consistent Writer has low variance

### Reputation & Offers
- Positive badges (Respected Veteran, Consummate Professional) = more battle offers
- Negative badges (Unreliable, Drama Starter) = fewer offers, worse matchups
- Media coverage changes based on your badges

### Bragging Rights
- Badge collection is your resume
- "I got 3 haymakers in one battle and unlocked Punchline King/Queen"
- "I went from Known Choker to Clutch Performer - redemption arc complete"

## How to Earn Badges

### Method 1: Performance Milestones
Hit specific thresholds during battles:
- **10 segments with peak_score ≥ 8.5** → Punchline King/Queen
- **5 battles won after being down 0-1 or 0-2** → Clutch Performer
- **10 battles without choking** → Resilient Battler
- **10 battles with consistency ≥ 0.85** → Consistent Writer

### Method 2: Playstyle Recognition
The game tracks your patterns over 10-15 battles:
- **15+ battles with ≥8 prep days** → Technical Writer
- **10+ battles won with ≤3 prep days** → Freestyle Genius
- **20+ battles with research ≥40% of total prep** → Angle Master
- **Use comedy content in 70%+ of battles** → Comedian (evolved from Comedy)

### Method 3: Career Progression
Experience and achievement-based:
- **20+ career battles + reputation ≥8** → Respected Veteran
- **30+ battles completed + high completion rate** → Consistent Grinder
- **All writing attributes ≥9** → Pen Game Elite
- **All performance attributes ≥9** → Stage Domination

### Method 4: Life Events
Choice-driven badges from life event decisions:
- **5+ professional/humble choices** → Consummate Professional
- **3+ drama-escalating choices** → Drama Starter
- **Random scandal event OR poor creativity** → Biter
- **Financial issues trigger** → Financial Struggles

### Method 5: Failure & Consequences
Negative badges earned through poor performance:
- **Choke in 2 consecutive battles** → Known Choker
- **1 no-show OR 2+ cancellations** → Unreliable
- **Low creativity + pattern detection** → Recycler
- **Use same content style for 15+ consecutive battles** → One-Trick Pony

## Badge Categories

## Writing & Content Badges

These badges define HOW you write your bars and craft your rounds.

### POSITIVE WRITING BADGES

#### Punchline King/Queen ⭐ (RARE)
**How to Earn**: Get 10 segments with peak_score ≥ 8.5 across your career

**Effects**:
- +15% peak segments (haymakers hit HARDER)
- +5 crowd reaction
- -50% consistency (you're flashy, not consistent)

**What It Means**: You're the battler who gets off 1-2 INSANE bars that have the crowd going nuts, but your average bars are just okay. You're hunting for that knockout punch every round.

**Playstyle**: Focus on big moments over consistency. Load up rounds with heavy punchlines even if filler suffers.

---

#### Scheme Specialist ⭐ (RARE)
**How to Earn**: 15+ battles with high lyricism (attribute 7+)

**Effects**:
- +25% lyricism
- +30% writing prep efficiency
- +1.0 consistency bonus (very consistent)
- High Prep Bonus (8+ days = extra boost)

**What It Means**: You're the battler who writes intricate multi-bar schemes. Preparation is your weapon. When you have 8+ prep days, you're dangerous.

**Playstyle**: Max out writing prep. Allocate 6-8+ days to writing. Your schemes need time to develop.

---

#### Wordplay Wizard ⭐ (RARE, Starter Option)
**How to Earn**: Choose at character creation OR build wordplay attribute to 8+

**Effects**:
- +40% wordplay (HUGE boost)
- +25% writing prep efficiency
- +8 crowd reaction

**What It Means**: You're the double entendre king/queen. Every bar has layers. Crowds love clever wordplay.

**Playstyle**: Focus writing prep on wordplay bars. Pairs well with Punchline King/Queen for devastating haymakers.

---

#### Freestyle Genius ⭐⭐⭐ (LEGENDARY, Starter Option)
**How to Earn**: 10+ battles won with ≤3 prep days OR choose at character creation

**Effects**:
- +30% creativity
- +20% peak bonus (rewards flashy freestyle moments)
- +15% rebuttal bonus when going second
- +20% research/performance prep efficiency
- -25% choke chance (freestyle is your safety net)
- -0.5% stumble reduction (good at recovery)
- Wild variance (1.5x segment variance multiplier)
- -1.5 consistency penalty (very inconsistent)
- Low Prep Bonus (3 days or less = +15% all stats)

**What It Means**: You're the battler who THRIVES on winging it. Minimal prep, maximum creativity. When you have 3 days or less prep, you get a +15% boost. You're clutch under pressure because freestyling is your comfort zone.

**Critical Note**: This badge was redesigned to reward LOW prep. Do NOT over-prepare. 0-3 days is your sweet spot.

**Playstyle**: Allocate 0-2 days to writing, focus on research (scenarios, not bars) and performance. Let your creativity flow in the moment.

**Synergies**: Rebuttal King/Queen, Comedy, Unorthodox
**Conflicts**: Technical Writer, Scheme Specialist, Overprepared, Consistent Writer

---

#### Technical Writer ⭐⭐⭐ (LEGENDARY, Starter Option)
**How to Earn**: 15+ battles with ≥8 prep days, writing-heavy focus

**Effects**:
- +35% writing prep efficiency
- +25% lyricism
- -10% stage presence (prep-focused, not performance)
- +1.0 consistency bonus
- +5% Small Room bonus (appreciated in intimate settings)
- High Prep Bonus (8+ days = extra boost)

**What It Means**: You're the battler who locks in for 10+ days and crafts ELITE bars. Every bar is intentional. You're consistent, technical, and deadly when fully prepped.

**Playstyle**: Max out writing prep. Aim for 8-10 days minimum. Sacrifice performance/life if needed - your pen is your weapon.

**Synergies**: Scheme Specialist, Pen Game Elite, Consistent Writer, Multisyllabic Master
**Conflicts**: Freestyle Genius, Underprepared, Lazy Writer

---

#### Angle Master ⭐⭐⭐ (LEGENDARY)
**How to Earn**: 20+ battles with research ≥40% of total prep

**Effects**:
- +35% research prep efficiency
- +20% peak bonus (angles land as haymakers)
- +20% creativity
- -10% wordplay (less about clever bars)
- -10 crowd reaction (overly analytical style can be boring)

**What It Means**: You're the battler who RESEARCHES opponents to death. Personal angles, deep digs, layered attacks. You dissect opponents with preparation.

**Playstyle**: Allocate 4-6 days to research every battle. Find personal angles. Pairs well with Personal Attacks badge.

**Synergies**: Personal Attacks, Battle Technician, Political Commentary
**Conflicts**: None direct, but crowds may not love overly analytical approaches

---

#### Pen Game Elite ⭐⭐⭐ (LEGENDARY)
**How to Earn**: All writing attributes ≥9 (Lyricism, Wordplay, Creativity)

**Effects**:
- +25% lyricism, wordplay, creativity (all writing attributes)
- +30% writing prep efficiency
- -10 crowd reaction (technical bars go over heads)
- High Prep Bonus (8+ days = extra boost)

**What It Means**: You're ELITE tier. Your writing is god-level. You're the battler other battlers study. But casual crowds might not appreciate your technical mastery.

**Playstyle**: This is endgame. Takes 30-40+ battles to reach. When you do, you're a certified legend.

**Synergies**: Technical Writer, Scheme Specialist, Multisyllabic Master
**Conflicts**: Biter (can't be elite if you're stealing)

### NEGATIVE WRITING BADGES

These badges hurt your performance. Most can be removed through improvement.

#### Known Choker ⚠️⚠️⚠️ (HIGH IMPACT NEGATIVE)
**How You Get It**: Choke in 2 consecutive battles

**Effects**:
- +7.0% choke chance per segment (46% choke rate per battle - BRUTAL)
- -40% rest efficiency
- -12 crowd reaction

**How to Remove**: 5 consecutive battles without choking

**What It Means**: You're known for freezing under pressure. Opponents target you. Crowds expect you to choke. This is career-damaging.

**Redemption Path**: Focus on rest days (3-4 per battle), avoid risky builds (Speed Rapping, Aggressive), build resilience attribute.

---

#### Biter ⚠️⚠️ (REPUTATION KILLER)
**How You Get It**: Caught stealing lines (scandal event) OR poor creativity despite wins

**Effects**:
- -40% creativity
- -15 crowd reaction
- -2 reputation (enforced in progression system)

**How to Remove**: 15 battles with creativity ≥8 + public redemption event (VERY HARD)

**What It Means**: You got caught stealing. Battle rap culture HATES biters. This is hard to recover from.

**Redemption Path**: Long-term. Build creativity attribute, avoid accusations, wait for redemption event.

---

#### Lazy Writer ⚠️ (Removable)
**How You Get It**: Consistently low writing prep (<2 days avg)

**Effects**:
- -40% writing prep efficiency
- -20% lyricism, wordplay, creativity

**How to Remove**: 10 battles with ≥6 prep days

**What It Means**: You're phoning it in. Not putting in the work.

**Redemption Path**: Start allocating 6+ days to writing. Show you care.

---

#### One-Trick Pony ⚠️ (Removable)
**How You Get It**: Use same content style for 15+ consecutive battles

**Effects**:
- -25% creativity
- +0.5 consistency bonus (at least predictable)
- Opponents get +10% prep bonus against you (enforced in battle sim)

**How to Remove**: Experiment with new content styles (7+ battles with different content)

**What It Means**: You're predictable. Every battle sounds the same. Opponents can game-plan for you.

**Redemption Path**: Try new content. If you're a comedy battler, try storytelling or personal attacks.

---

#### Other Negative Writing Badges

**Recycler**: -30% creativity, -20% writing prep, -10 crowd (remove: 8 battles with creativity ≥7)

**Reach God/Goddess**: -30% wordplay, -8 crowd, -10% peak (forced wordplay doesn't land)

**Filler Abuser**: -20% lyricism, -10 crowd, -15% peak (too much filler dilutes haymakers)

**Outdated Referencer**: -15% creativity, -12 crowd, -20% research prep (references don't land)

**Predictable Rhymer**: -15% lyricism, -30% creativity, +0.5 consistency

**Weak Punchline Setups**: -15% peak, -15% lyricism (can't land big moments)

**Shallow Research**: -50% research prep, -10% creativity

**Redundant**: -20% lyricism, -10 crowd, -20% peak (saying same thing multiple ways)

**Overcomplicated**: +10% lyricism but -18 crowd, -10% delivery (too complex for audience)

**Cliche Abuser**: -30% creativity, -15% wordplay, -8 crowd (overused phrases)

**Name Flip Dependent**: +10% wordplay but -25% creativity, -12 crowd (gimmicky)

## Badge Effects Explained

### Understanding Effect Types

#### Multipliers (Stack Multiplicatively)
- **Example**: +25% lyricism badge + +15% lyricism badge = 1.25 × 1.15 = 1.4375 (43.75% total)
- **Applies to**: Attribute multipliers, prep efficiency

#### Bonuses/Penalties (Stack Additively)
- **Example**: +15 crowd reaction badge + +10 crowd reaction badge = +25 total
- **Applies to**: Crowd reaction, choke chance, consistency

#### Choke Mechanics
- **Base choke rate**: 1.5% per segment (7% avg per battle)
- **Known Choker**: +7.0% per segment (46% per battle)
- **Clutch Performer**: -4.0% per segment (3% per battle)
- **Calculation**: Per-segment, not per-battle

#### Peak vs Average vs Consistency
- **Peak Score**: Your best segment (the "haymaker")
- **Average Score**: Mean across all segments
- **Consistency Score**: Based on standard deviation (lower variance = higher consistency)

**Example Builds**:

**Flashy Build**: Punchline King/Queen + Freestyle Genius
- High peak, low consistency = "He had 2 CRAZY bars but was weak overall"

**Consistent Build**: Technical Writer + Consistent Writer
- High average, high consistency = "Solid 3 rounds, no weak moments"

**Balanced Build**: Battle Technician + Scheme Specialist
- Good peak, good average, good consistency = "Complete performance"

## Badge Synergies & Conflicts

### What Are Synergies?

When you have badges that work WELL together, you get +5% effectiveness per synergy.

**Example**:
- You have: Technical Writer + Scheme Specialist + Pen Game Elite
- Synergies: 3 badges all boost each other
- Bonus: +10-15% effectiveness (multiple synergies stacking)

### What Are Conflicts?

When you have badges that work POORLY together, you get -8% effectiveness per conflict + +1% choke per conflict.

**Example**:
- You have: Freestyle Genius + Technical Writer
- Conflict: Philosophy clash (improvise vs over-prepare)
- Penalty: -8% effectiveness, +1% choke

**Critical Conflicts to Avoid**:

1. **Freestyle Genius + Technical Writer**: -8% effectiveness, +1% choke (identity crisis)
2. **Aggressive + Comedy**: -8% effectiveness (can't be intimidating and funny)
3. **Pen Game Elite + Biter**: -12% effectiveness (elite reputation tarnished)
4. **Clutch Performer + Known Choker**: -8% effectiveness (inconsistent under pressure)

---

#### Writing Synergies
- **Technical Writer + Scheme Specialist + Pen Game Elite + Consistent Writer**: +20% effectiveness (elite writing package)
- **Freestyle Genius + Rebuttal King/Queen + Comedy**: +10% effectiveness (improvisation masters)
- **Wordplay Wizard + Punchline King/Queen + Multisyllabic Master**: +10% effectiveness (haymaker build)

## Negative Badges & Redemption

### Philosophy of Redemption

Battle rap is about STORIES. Negative badges are plot twists. Redemption arcs are satisfying.

**You can remove most negative badges through consistent improvement.**

---

### Redemption Difficulty Tiers

#### EASY TO REMOVE (5-10 battles)

**Known Choker** → 5 consecutive battles without choking
- Strategy: Focus on rest (3-4 days per battle), build resilience to 7-8, avoid risky badges

**Drama Starter** → 5 consecutive drama-free life event choices
- Strategy: Choose mature/professional options in life events, avoid beefs

**Inconsistent Performer** → 5 consecutive consistent performances (consistency ≥0.75)
- Strategy: Balanced prep, avoid risky builds, focus on consistency over peaks

**Lazy Writer** → 10 battles with ≥6 prep days
- Strategy: Allocate 6-8 days to writing, show you care

**Recycler** → 8 consecutive battles with creativity ≥7
- Strategy: Build creativity attribute, use diverse content

---

#### MEDIUM DIFFICULTY (10-20 battles)

**One-Trick Pony** → 7+ battles with different content styles
- Strategy: Experiment. If you're a comedy battler, try storytelling or angles

**Clout Chaser** → Build reputation ≥7 through authentic choices
- Strategy: Make professional choices, focus on substance over viral moments

**Career Plateaued** → Improve 2+ attributes by 1 point each
- Strategy: Focus on attribute progression (performance-based gains)

**Controversial** → 5 humble/professional choices
- Strategy: Tone down shock value, make mature choices

**Sore Loser** → Handle 3 losses gracefully (choice-based)
- Strategy: Choose humble/respectful options when losing

---

#### HARD TO REMOVE (20-30 battles)

**Unreliable** → 20 consecutive battle completions + reputation recovery
- Strategy: NEVER miss a battle. Show up to everything. Build trust back.

**Biter** → 15 battles with creativity ≥8 + public redemption event
- Strategy: Build creativity to 8-9, wait for redemption event trigger

**Backstabber** → 25 battles + no drama + loyalty events
- Strategy: Long-term. Avoid beefs, make loyal choices

**Disrespectful** → 15 respectful choices + public apology event
- Strategy: Make respectful choices, wait for apology event

**Bitter Veteran** → Attitude shift (choice pattern change)
- Strategy: Stop making negative/resentful choices

---

#### VERY HARD / PERMANENT

**Known Stealer** → CANNOT BE REMOVED (permanent reputation damage)
- This is career-ending. Avoid at all costs.

**Fallen Star** → Represents career peak that's past (narrative permanence)
- Cannot be removed. This is your legacy.

**Washed** → Attribute recovery (extremely rare, near-impossible)
- You'd need to rebuild all attributes after decline. Very hard.

---

### How to Avoid Negative Badges

#### Don't Choke Repeatedly
- Allocate 3-4 rest days per battle
- Build resilience attribute to 7-8
- Avoid risky badges (Speed Rapping +1.5% choke, Aggressive +1% choke)
- Avoid low prep if you don't have Freestyle Genius

#### Never No-Show
- ALWAYS show up to accepted battles
- Unreliable badge = -40% battle offers (career killer)

#### Make Professional Choices
- Choose mature/humble options in life events
- Avoid drama, beefs, controversy (unless building Controversial on purpose)

#### Diversify Content
- Don't use same content 15+ battles in a row (One-Trick Pony)
- Experiment with new styles every 10-15 battles

#### Don't Steal
- Biter badge is HARD to remove
- Build creativity attribute legitimately
