# Life Events Evaluation & Tuning Report

**Date**: November 30, 2025
**Purpose**: Evaluate current life events system against battle rap authenticity research
**Audience**: Development team preparing for YouTube launch

---

## Executive Summary

### Overall Life Events Authenticity Score: **6.5/10**

The current life events system has a **solid technical foundation** but suffers from **generic storytelling** that doesn't capture the specific culture of battle rap. While the mechanics work (choices, effects, triggers), the events themselves feel like they could be from any competitive activity rather than the unique world of battle rap.

**Current Status**: 18 choice-based events covering career moments, personal struggles, and battle outcomes. Mechanically sound, culturally bland.

**Critical Findings**:
- ✅ **Mechanics are excellent** - Three-tier system (passive/choice/triggered) is well-designed
- ✅ **Effects are balanced** - Attribute changes feel fair and impactful
- ❌ **Missing battle rap-specific culture** - No Twitter beefs, viral moments, league politics, or media drama
- ❌ **No choke redemption arcs** - Choking is career-defining in battle rap, but events don't capture this
- ❌ **No day job dynamics** - Most battlers work 9-5s, but this isn't reflected
- ❌ **No league blackball/favoritism** - League politics are HUGE in battle rap

**Recommended Path**:
1. **Keep existing events** (they work mechanically)
2. **Add 15-20 battle rap-specific events** (Week 1-2 priority)
3. **Retune trigger rates** (Week 2)
4. **Add event chaining** (Post-launch)

## Part 2: Battle Rap Authenticity Analysis

### What Real Battlers Deal With (From Research)

Real battle rap culture involves:

1. **Twitter/Social Media Beefs** - Constant callouts, subliminals, viral tweets
2. **Choking as Career-Defining** - One choke can label you forever (Aye Verb, Conceited)
3. **League Politics** - Getting blackballed, favored, or screwed by leagues (URL, KOTD, RBE)
4. **Financial Struggles** - Can't afford to travel to big leagues, turning down URL cards
5. **Day Job Balance** - Battling while working 9-5, boss threatening to fire you
6. **Media Drama** - Blogs stirring controversy, podcast comments blowing up
7. **YouTube Reactions** - Your bars getting broken down by reactors, going viral
8. **Rap Career Crossover** - Label interest, trying to transition to music
9. **Venue Politics** - Small room vs main stage dynamics, league progression
10. **Coaching/Mentorship** - Learning from vets (DNA, Loaded Lux, etc.)
11. **Health Issues** - Mental health, addiction, physical health affecting performance
12. **Relationship Drama** - Breakups while on the road, groupies, fame affecting personal life
13. **Viral Moments** - One line going viral, memes, "ZIP EM UP" type moments
14. **Choke Redemption Arcs** - Fighting the "choker" label for years
15. **League Feuds** - URL vs KOTD beef, battlers taking sides

## Part 5: Event Tuning Recommendations

### Current Event Frequency Issues

#### Too Rare (Need Higher Trigger Rate)

**1. Choke Events (Currently trigger only on choke)**
- **Problem**: Chokes are rare (7% avg), so most players never see these events
- **Impact**: Career-defining moments are missed
- **Recommendation**:
  - Keep choke-triggered events
  - Add "close call" events (stumble but didn't full choke)
  - Add "choke anxiety" events (triggered by stress, not actual choke)

**2. Controversial Decision (2-1 only)**
- **Problem**: Only fires on 2-1 wins, which are ~40% of battles
- **Impact**: Good event is underused
- **Recommendation**: Also trigger on 2-1 losses with close crowd reaction

**3. Media Interview (Reputation ≥7 only)**
- **Problem**: Most players won't reach rep 7 early
- **Impact**: Media layer is invisible to new players
- **Recommendation**: Add lower-tier "local blog" event at rep 4+

---

#### Too Common (Need Lower Trigger Rate / Cooldown)

**1. "Any Battle" Triggers (Family Wedding, Injury, Training Partner, Venue Change)**
- **Problem**: No trigger condition = could fire every battle
- **Impact**: Feels repetitive, not special
- **Recommendation**:
  - Add 3-5 battle cooldown
  - Add probability (50% chance even if condition met)
  - Make these "random" life events rarer

**2. Win Streak Events (3 and 5 streaks)**
- **Problem**: Fire every time you hit the streak
- **Impact**: Becomes predictable
- **Recommendation**: Add "can_trigger_multiple_times: false" (one-time events)

---

#### Perfect Frequency (Don't Touch)

**1. Dominant Victory / Bodybag Hype**
- **Trigger**: 3-0 wins
- **Frequency**: ~20-30% of battles (good)
- **Status**: ✅ Keep as-is

**2. Controversial Loss / Robbery**
- **Trigger**: 2-1 loss + close crowd
- **Frequency**: ~10-15% of battles (good)
- **Status**: ✅ Keep as-is

### Consequence Tuning

#### Effects That Feel Too Strong

**1. Financial Stability Swings**

- **Example**: "Close Victory" gives +0.1 reputation

- **Recommendation**: Minimum reputation change should be ±0.3

**2. Resilience Changes**

- **Impact**: Choke chance reduction is negligible
- **Recommendation**: Resilience should change by ±0.5 minimum

- **Recommendation**: Test if prep bonuses actually affect outcomes meaningfully

### Suggested SQL Queries for Analytics

**1. Event Frequency by Template**
```sql
SELECT
  template_code,
  COUNT(*) as times_triggered,
  AVG(CASE WHEN status = 'resolved' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) as resolution_rate
FROM battler_life_events
GROUP BY template_code
ORDER BY times_triggered DESC;
```

**2. Choice Distribution**
```sql
SELECT
  template_code,
  choice_selected,
  COUNT(*) as times_chosen
FROM battler_life_events
WHERE status = 'resolved'
GROUP BY template_code, choice_selected
ORDER BY template_code, times_chosen DESC;
```

**3. Events by Battler Type**
```sql
-- Requires join with battler_attributes to determine writer/performer/balanced
SELECT
  CASE
    WHEN writing_avg > performance_avg + 1.5 THEN 'writer'
    WHEN performance_avg > writing_avg + 1.5 THEN 'performer'
    ELSE 'balanced'
  END as battler_type,
  ble.template_code,
  COUNT(*) as times_triggered
FROM battler_life_events ble
JOIN battler_attributes ba ON ble.battler_id = ba.battler_id
GROUP BY battler_type, template_code;
```

**4. Event Impact on Win Rate**
```sql
-- Compare win rate before/after specific events
WITH event_battles AS (
  SELECT
    battler_id,
    template_code,
    triggered_at
  FROM battler_life_events
  WHERE template_code = 'CHOKE_REDEMPTION_ARC_START'
),
before_after_wins AS (
  SELECT
    eb.battler_id,
    eb.template_code,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN b.created_at < eb.triggered_at AND b.winner_id = eb.battler_id THEN 1 END) as wins_before,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN b.created_at >= eb.triggered_at AND b.winner_id = eb.battler_id THEN 1 END) as wins_after,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN b.created_at < eb.triggered_at THEN 1 END) as battles_before,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN b.created_at >= eb.triggered_at THEN 1 END) as battles_after
  FROM event_battles eb
  JOIN battles b ON b.battler_player_id = eb.battler_id OR b.battler_ai_id = eb.battler_id
  GROUP BY eb.battler_id, eb.template_code
)
SELECT
  template_code,
  AVG(wins_before::FLOAT / NULLIF(battles_before, 0)) as win_rate_before,
  AVG(wins_after::FLOAT / NULLIF(battles_after, 0)) as win_rate_after
FROM before_after_wins
GROUP BY template_code;
```
