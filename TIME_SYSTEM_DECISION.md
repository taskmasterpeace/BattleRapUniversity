## RESEARCH FINDINGS: THE BEST APPROACH

### Recommended: **HYBRID "CIRCUIT SEASONS" SYSTEM**

**How It Works:**

```
SHARED LAYER (Real Calendar):
├─ Seasons: 3-month competitive circuits
├─ Weekly events: "Grudge Match Weekend" (Fri-Sun)
├─ Monthly tournaments: Championship brackets
└─ AI-generated media: Updates throughout

PERSONAL LAYER (Action-Based):
├─ Prep Phase: 7 "days" = 14 time slots (instant, no waiting)
├─ Life Events: Triggered by actions, not calendar
├─ Career Days: Advance through YOUR actions
└─ Training: Optional passive progression (offline)

INSTANCED BATTLES:
├─ Matchmaking from global pool (any career day)
├─ Battle happens in "neutral space"
├─ Both players prep async
└─ Simulate when both ready (or deadline)
```

### Why This Solves Your Concerns:

**1. "Years go by too fast"**
- Career days only advance when YOU do things (prep, life events, battles)
- Sitting idle = time doesn't pass
- Natural pacing from action economy

**2. "How does multiplayer sync?"**
- Player A on Day 50 vs Player B on Day 10 = NO PROBLEM
- Battles are "instanced" outside career timeline
- ELO rating exists above individual timelines
- You battle based on skill, not career day count

**3. "Battles should feel like events"**
- Optional: Weekend battles get 2x rewards (creates rhythm)
- Prep phase creates anticipation (5 days of choices before battle)
- Media articles generate hype between battles

**4. "How do life events work?"**
- Deadlines = "72 hours from trigger" (duration-based)
- Not "72 hours on the calendar"
- Works across time zones

## THE THREE OPTIONS

### Option A: Pure Action-Based (Persona 5 Style)
```
Every action = time passes
14 prep slots per battle
No real-time waiting
Career advances at your pace
```
**Best for:** Single-player focus, mobile sessions
**Downside:** Less "live" multiplayer feel

### Option B: Real-Time Hybrid (Your Weekend Idea)
```
Battles only happen Fri-Sun
Prep phase = Mon-Thu
Life events = anytime
Weekly cycle mirrors real battle rap
```
**Best for:** Hardcore players, community events
**Downside:** Can't play battles mid-week

### Option C: Session-Based (Hades Style)
```
Each play session = 1 "week"
30-min session = prep + battle + aftermath
Self-contained loops
Progress saved between sessions
```
**Best for:** Busy players, mobile
**Downside:** Less "living world" feel

---

## MY RECOMMENDATION: Option A with Weekend Bonuses

**Core:** Pure action-based (time only passes when you act)
**Enhancement:** Weekend battles give 2x rep/rewards
**Result:** Play anytime, but weekends are special

**Why:**
1. No waiting frustration
2. Multiplayer works across all timezones
3. Casual and hardcore players both served
4. Weekend bonus creates natural rhythm without forcing it
