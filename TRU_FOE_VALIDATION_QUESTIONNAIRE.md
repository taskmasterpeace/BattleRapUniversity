# Battle Rap Mechanics Validation - Expert Interview
## For Tru Foe

## SECTION 1: PERFORMANCE ERRORS & CHOKING

### Question 1: Stumble vs Choke Frequency
In our game, we distinguish between:
- **Stumbles**: Minor errors (forgot a line, slight hesitation, minor slip-up)
- **Chokes**: Complete breakdowns (major memory loss, can't recover)

**Current Settings**:
- ~25% of battles have at least one battler stumble
- ~5% of battles have at least one battler choke completely (average battler)
- Battlers known for choking: ~25% choke rate

**Questions**:
1. Does 1 in 4 battles having someone stumble feel right?
2. Is 1 in 20 battles having a complete choke accurate for average battlers?
3. For battlers with a reputation for choking (like early Hitman Holla moments), does 1 in 4 battles sound right?
4. Are there battlers you'd say choke more than 25% of the time?

### Question 2: Error Severity
**Current Settings**:
- Stumble = 30% performance penalty (or 15% if you recover well)
- Choke = 70% performance penalty

**Questions**:
1. When someone stumbles but recovers, how much does it hurt them? (We're saying 15-30% worse than normal)
2. When someone chokes completely, can they still win that round? (We're making it nearly impossible with 70% penalty)
3. Have you seen battles where someone choked a round but still won it? How rare is that?

### Question 3: Recovery Mechanics
**Current Settings**: Elite performers (8+ delivery/crowd control) reduce stumble penalty from 30% → 15%

**Questions**:
1. Do top-tier performers like Hollow, Lux, or Nitty recover from stumbles better than mid-tier battlers?
2. Is crowd control and stage presence the key to recovering from errors, or is it something else?
3. Can you think of specific battles where someone stumbled but recovered so well it barely mattered?
