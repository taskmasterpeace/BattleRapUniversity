**Pen Gamers:** Lux Coded, JC the Titan, Jones Chilla, Magic B, Cortez, P Mike
**Performers:** Roc Tay, Surf Tsu, Holla Hitman, Goodz, Hallow The Dawn

**Strategy:**
- **Pen Gamers:** Force Small Room venue (writing weight 60%), complex schemes
- **Performers:** Force Main Stage venue (performance weight 50%), high energy

**HIGH Street Cred:** Nitty Rum, Gotti Geechi, Red O, Will Ill, Foe Tru, DNA
