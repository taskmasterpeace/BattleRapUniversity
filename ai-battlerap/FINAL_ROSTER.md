## STRATEGIC INSIGHTS

### God Tier Counters
- **The Architect:** Maximize performance (he's stage 9 not 10)
- **Tsunami Wave:** Exploit resilience 7 (choke risk) and out-write (8s not 10s)
- **The Nitro Puncher:** Target stage presence 6 (glass cannon)
- **The Comedian:** No clear weakness - must out-execute

### Exploitable Weaknesses
- **Low Resilience:** Daybreak Lit (6), Punch Wizard (6), Island Puzzle (6), Professional Prep (6)
- **Low Preparation:** Money Talk God (6), Daybreak Lit (6), Hollow Victory (6), Punch Wizard (6)
- **Low Stage Presence:** The Titan Scribe (5), Island Puzzle (4), Strategy Chess (5)

### League Advantages
- **Small Room Circuit:** Favors writers (The Architect, Compton Kingpin, Daybreak Lit)
- **Main Stage Arena:** Favors performers (Tsunami Wave, Baltimore Rocker, Showtime Holla)
