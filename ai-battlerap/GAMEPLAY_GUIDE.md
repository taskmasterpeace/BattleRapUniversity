## GAME OVERVIEW

### What is Battle Rap University?

Battle Rap University is a strategic battle rap simulation game where you create a battler, prepare for matchups against AI opponents, and compete in a realistic battle rap ecosystem. **You don't write actual lyrics**—instead, you manage your battler's attributes, choose your daily prep focus, and watch segment-by-segment simulations unfold. It's a blend of management strategy, stat progression, and battle rap authenticity.

### The Core Gameplay Loop

Each session follows a natural rhythm:

1. **Accept Battle Offers** - AI opponents challenge you based on your skill level
2. **Prepare (7-14 days)** - Choose daily focus: research (studying angles), writing (crafting bars), performance (stage presence), rest (managing stress), or life (personal balance)
3. **Battle Day** - Watch your battle unfold segment-by-segment, seeing your peaks (haymaker moments), consistency, crowd reaction, and whether you choke
4. **Progression** - Earn reputation, improve attributes, unlock badges, face harder opponents

### Victory & Progression

Battles are best-of-three rounds. Win more than you lose, and your ELO rating climbs. You'll unlock harder opponents, earn badges that define your battler's archetype, and accumulate reputation in the battle rap community. There's no final "endgame"—progression is cyclical. **The real victory is building a battler with a coherent identity and watching them dominate their league.**

### Key Features

- **Two Distinct Leagues**:
  - **Small Room Circuit** - Rewards technical writing (2-min rounds, intimate crowds)
  - **Main Stage Arena** - Favors performance and crowd control (3-min rounds, energy-driven)

- **Badge System**: Earn badges like "Pen Game Elite" (technical writer), "Freestyle Genius" (improviser), or "Performance Beast" (crowd controller). These aren't cosmetic—they change how you prep and battle.

- **Prep Matters**: Your daily focus directly impacts battle performance. Skipping prep gets punished. High stress increases your choke probability.

- **Realistic Simulation**: Battles show moment-by-moment variance. You might have a dominant third round but weak consistency overall—just like real battle rap.

- **Life Events**: Between battles, you'll face controversies, viral moments, personal crises that alter your trajectory.

### Time Commitment

A typical session takes **10-20 minutes**: accept offers, plan your weekly prep (2-3 minutes), then check back when the battle's scheduled to view results. Between sessions, the game runs in the background—new offers arrive, stress accumulates or recovers based on your choices.

**Stage Name** - Pick something memorable! Examples: "Pattern Master", "Stage Commander", "The Surgeon"

**Primary League** - This is crucial! Choose between:
- **Small Room Circuit** - Best for writers and technical battlers
- **Main Stage Arena** - Best for performers and crowd workers

### Step 2: Understanding Attributes (1-10 Scale)

You have **four attribute categories**:

#### Writing Attributes (affects bars, complexity, creativity):
- **Lyricism** - Word choice, metaphor depth, poetic quality
- **Wordplay** - Double meanings, puns, clever setups
- **Creativity** - Unique angles, unexpected approaches

#### Performance Attributes (affects stage presence, energy):
- **Stage Presence** - Command of the room, confidence
- **Crowd Control** - Ability to hype the audience

#### Personal Attributes (affects life events, stability):
- **Financial Stability** - Money management, sponsor deals
- **Reputation** - How the community views you
- **Family Bond** - Personal support system

#### Resilience (separate stat):
- **Resilience** - Ability to handle pressure, resist choking

**Attribute Tiers:**
- **Low (1-3)** - Struggling, noticeable weakness
- **Mid (4-6)** - Competent, average level
- **Top (7-9)** - Exceptional, recognized strength

### Step 3: Choosing Your League

This decision shapes your entire career:

#### Small Room Circuit
- **2-minute rounds** (4 segments per round)
- **60% writing weight, 40% performance weight**
- **Lower crowd factor** (crowd reactions matter less)
- **Best for**: Technical writers, complex schemes, wordplay masters

- **3-minute rounds** (6 segments per round)
- **40% writing weight, 60% performance weight**
- **Higher crowd factor** (crowd reactions matter more)
- **Best for**: Performers, crowd workers, high-energy battlers
- **Real-life parallel**: URL main events, big stages

**Important**: You can switch leagues, but specialists dominate. A Technical Writer might win 60-70% in Small Room but only 30-40% in Main Stage.

## THE PREPARATION PHASE

**This is the core of the game.** Every battle has a 7-14 day prep window. What you do during this time determines your performance.

### Daily Focus Options

Each day, you choose ONE focus:

#### 1. **RESEARCH** (Studying Your Opponent)
**What it does:**
- Increases Creativity by 0.05 per day
- Increases Lyricism by 0.03 per day
- Unlocks "angles" (personal information to exploit)
- Doubles your chance of hitting "haymaker" moments (big peaks)

**Best for**: Angle Masters, battle technicians, anyone who wants big moments

**Example**: "You spend the day watching your opponent's old battles, taking notes on patterns and weaknesses. You discover he always starts slow—you can capitalize on that."

---

#### 2. **WRITING** (Crafting Your Bars)
**What it does:**
- Increases Lyricism, Wordplay, AND Creativity by 0.10 each per day
- Most direct stat boost for writers
- Stacks with badge efficiency (Technical Writer gets 1.35x multiplier!)

**Best for**: Technical Writers, anyone in Small Room Circuit

**Example**: "You lock yourself in a room with a notepad, crafting complex multi-syllable schemes and layered metaphors."

#### 3. **PERFORMANCE** (Stage Work)
**What it does:**
- Increases Stage Presence, Crowd Control, AND Delivery by 0.10 each per day

**Best for**: Performance Beasts, anyone in Main Stage Arena

**What it does:**
- Increases Resilience by 0.10 per day
- Reduces stress accumulation

- Prevents burnout

**Best for**: Everyone needs this! Especially after consecutive battles

**Example**: "You take a day off, hang with friends, sleep in. When you wake up, your mind is sharper."

### How Prep Affects Your Battle

**The Math (Simplified):**
- Base stat (let's say Lyricism = 7)
- Add prep gains (5 writing days = +0.50)
- Apply badge multipliers (Technical Writer 1.35x = ×1.35)
- Apply pattern bonuses (8+ days prep = +12%)
- **Final Lyricism: 7 + 0.50 = 7.50 × 1.35 × 1.12 = 11.34 → capped at 10**

**Translation**: Good prep can take you from "good" (7) to "god tier" (10) for that battle!

---

#### Freestyle Genius (3-day prep - Low Prep Bonus!):
- Day 1-2: **Research** (prep scenarios, not bars)

## HOW BATTLES WORK

### Battle Structure

Every battle is **best-of-three rounds**. Win 2 rounds, you win the battle.

**Small Room Circuit**: 2-minute rounds = 4 segments per round
**Main Stage Arena**: 3-minute rounds = 6 segments per round

Each **segment** is a 30-second chunk that gets scored independently.

### What You See During a Battle

The battle viewer shows you:
- **Segment-by-segment scores** for both battlers
- **Round summaries** (average score, peak score, consistency, crowd reaction)
- **Special moments** (haymakers, chokes, momentum shifts)
- **Final result** (who won which rounds, overall winner)

### How Scoring Works (Behind the Scenes)

**Each Segment Gets Scored Like This:**

1. **Calculate Base Writing Power**
   - Average your 3 writing stats (Lyricism, Wordplay, Creativity)
   - Example: (9 + 9 + 8) / 3 = 8.67

2. **Calculate Base Performance Power**
   - Average your 3 performance stats (Stage Presence, Crowd Control, Delivery)
   - Example: (5 + 5 + 6) / 3 = 5.33

3. **Apply League Weights**
   - Small Room: (8.67 × 0.60) + (5.33 × 0.40) = 5.20 + 2.13 = **7.33**
   - Main Stage: (8.67 × 0.40) + (5.33 × 0.60) = 3.47 + 3.20 = **6.67**

   **See the difference?** Same attributes, but Technical Writer (8.67 writing) scores **7.33 in Small Room** vs **6.67 in Main Stage**!

4. **Add Random Variance**
   - Each segment gets ±25% variance
   - Creates unpredictability (7.33 can become 5.50-9.16)
   - This is why you have "off" segments and "amazing" segments

5. **Check for Special Moments**
   - **Haymaker** (15% chance, 20% if you did research): Score × 1.2-1.4
   - **Choke** (3% base, modified by resilience/prep): Score × 0.3

### Round Scoring

After all segments, the game calculates:

- **Average Score** - Mean of all your segments (PRIMARY metric)
- **Peak Score** - Your best segment (the "haymaker")
- **Consistency Score** - How close your segments were (low variance = consistent)
- **Crowd Reaction** - Based on performance stats + league crowd factor

**Round Winner** = Higher average score (or peak score if tied)

### Battle Winner

Win 2 out of 3 rounds = Battle victory!

### Why This Matters

**Real Battle Rap Outcomes:**
- You can have ONE amazing round (peak 9.5) but lose 1-2 overall
- Consistency beats flashiness (three 7.5 rounds beats 9.5, 6.0, 6.0)
- The crowd doesn't always pick the winner (average score does)
- Choking ONE segment can cost you the round

## LIFE EVENTS

Between battles, **life events** happen. These are narrative turning points that affect your stats, reputation, and career trajectory.

### Event Types & Trigger Rates

- **Random Events** (10% chance per day) - Unexpected situations
- **Battle Result Events** (20% after wins/losses) - Reactions to your performance
- **Time-Based Events** (15% on certain days) - Calendar-triggered
- **Attribute-Based Events** (25% when stats hit thresholds) - Milestone reactions

**Pacing**: Maximum 1 event per 3 days (prevents spam)

### Example Life Events

- "Your battle goes viral - 1 million views!"
- "A sponsor approaches you for a deal"
- "Drama with another battler starts on Twitter"
- "You choked badly and it's trending"
- "Family emergency requires your attention"
- "You're invited to a prestigious tournament"

### Making Choices

Every life event gives you **5 options**:

#### 1. **COMPOSED** (60% success rate)
**Who benefits**: Technical Writers, Battle Technicians
**Example**: "Handle it professionally, release a statement"
**Effect**: Safe, moderate gains, maintains reputation

#### 2. **RISKY** (45% success rate)
**Who benefits**: Controversial battlers, Drama Starters
**Example**: "Double down, challenge them to a battle"
**Effect**: High reward if it works, serious penalties if it fails

#### 3. **IMPROVISED** (50% base, 75% for Freestyle Genius!)
**Who benefits**: Freestylers, creative battlers
**Example**: "Freestyle a response and post it immediately"
**Effect**: Creativity-based, Freestylers get huge advantage

#### 4. **HUMBLE** (65% success rate)
**Who benefits**: Long-term reputation builders
**Example**: "Apologize, take the L, move on gracefully"
**Effect**: Best success rate, long-term reputation gains

#### 5. **TECHNICAL** (55% success rate, attribute-dependent)
**Who benefits**: High-attribute specialists
**Example**: "Break down the situation with facts and logic"
**Effect**: Success depends on your relevant attributes

### How Badges Affect Choices

**Freestyle Genius**: +25% success on Improvised choices
**Performance Beast**: +15% success on Risky choices (crowd loves the drama)
**Technical Writer**: +10% success on Composed choices
**Angle Master**: +15% success on Technical choices

### Example Event: "Your Battle Goes Viral"

**Situation**: "Your last battle just hit 500K views on YouTube overnight. Everyone's talking about your third round. How do you capitalize?"

**Option A (Composed)**: Post a humble thank-you, announce your next battle
- **Success**: +0.5 Reputation, +1 Financial Stability, +15 Public Knowledge
- **Failure**: Looks calculated, +5 Public Knowledge only

**Option B (Risky)**: Call out the top-ranked battler in your league
- **Success**: +1.0 Reputation, Battle offer from top-ranked, +30 Public Knowledge
- **Failure**: -1.0 Reputation, -0.5 Family Bond (stress), "Drama Starter" badge risk

**Option C (Improvised)**: Freestyle a victory lap on social media
- **Success (base 50%, 75% for Freestyle Genius)**: +0.8 Reputation, +25 Public Knowledge, crowd loves it
- **Failure**: Looks try-hard, -0.3 Reputation

**Option D (Humble)**: Thank your opponent, praise the crowd, stay grounded
- **Success (65%)**: +0.6 Reputation, +0.3 Family Bond, long-term respect
- **Failure**: Minimal loss, +0.2 Reputation anyway

**Option E (Technical)**: Analyze your own performance professionally
- **Success (55%, higher if Lyricism 8+)**: +0.7 Reputation, "Battle Technician" badge progress
- **Failure**: Comes off as robotic, no change

Badges are modifiers that:
- Increase/decrease prep efficiency
- Boost/penalize specific attributes
- Change choke probability
- Affect crowd reactions
- Create synergies or conflicts with other badges

**Example**: "Pen Game Elite" gives +25% Lyricism BUT -10 Crowd Reaction (technical bars go over heads)

## STRATEGY GUIDE

### Beginner Tips (Battles 1-5)

1. **Start Balanced** - Don't specialize immediately. Learn how prep affects battles.
2. **Prep EVERY Battle** - Even bad prep beats no prep. Aim for 7 days minimum.
3. **Small Room First** - More forgiving, less crowd pressure, learn the basics.
4. **Save After Wins** - Build a win streak before taking risks.
5. **Choose Composed** - In life events, pick the safe option until you understand mechanics.

### Intermediate Tips (Battles 6-20)

1. **Specialize Your Build** - Pick Technical Writer, Performance Beast, or Freestyle Genius and commit.
2. **Learn League Matchups** - Technical Writers dominate Small Room (60% writing weight), Performance Beasts dominate Main Stage (60% performance weight).
3. **Manage Life Events** - Use events to reinforce your archetype (Technical Writers pick Composed, Freestylers pick Improvised).
4. **Stack Synergies** - Get 3+ badges that synergize for +15% prep efficiency.
5. **Track Your Opponents** - Note which AI battlers you've beaten/lost to.

### Advanced Tips (Battles 21+)

1. **Counter-Building** - If facing a Performance Beast, switch to Small Room. If facing Technical Writer, switch to Main Stage.
2. **Min-Max Prep** - Understand diminishing returns. 8 writing days is the sweet spot (high prep bonus triggers).
3. **Badge Synergy Stacking** - 4+ synergistic badges gives +20-25% prep efficiency.
4. **Reputation Management** - Avoid negative badges unless you're deliberately building a "Controversial" career path.
5. **Stress Cycling** - Take 1 rest day every 3-4 battles to prevent burnout and keep choke % low.

### Common Mistakes

❌ **Ignoring Prep** - "I have 9 Lyricism, I don't need to prep!" WRONG. Prep matters more than raw stats.
❌ **Taking All Risky Choices** - You'll tank your reputation and accumulate negative badges.
❌ **Neglecting Rest** - Consecutive battles without rest = stress = higher choke chance.
❌ **Fighting in Wrong League** - Technical Writer in Main Stage = 30% win rate. Know your league!
❌ **No Synergies** - Random badges with no plan = wasted badge slots.

### Archetype-Specific Strategies

#### Technical Writer
✅ **Do**: Prep 8-10 days (writing-heavy), fight in Small Room, stack writing badges
❌ **Don't**: Fight in Main Stage without high prep, take risky life event choices

#### Performance Beast
✅ **Do**: Prep 7 days (performance-heavy), fight in Main Stage, engage with crowds
❌ **Don't**: Neglect writing entirely (you need SOME bars), fight in Small Room

#### Freestyle Genius
✅ **Do**: Research-heavy prep (3-5 days), embrace variance, take improvised choices
❌ **Don't**: Over-prep (you LOSE your Low Prep Bonus!), avoid conflicts with Technical Writer badges

#### Angle Master
✅ **Do**: Research 6+ days, go for big peak moments, exploit discovered secrets
❌ **Don't**: Expect crowd love (you have -10 crowd), skip research prep
