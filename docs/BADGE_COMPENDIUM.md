# Badge Assignment Guide

## GOD TIER Battlers (3-5 badges)
- 2-3 Legendary/Epic positive badges
- 0-1 weakness badge for realism
- Example: Lux = `bar_god`, `metaphor_master`, `technical_writer`, `respected_veteran`

## TOP TIER Battlers (3-4 badges)
- 1-2 Epic positive badges
- 1-2 Rare positive badges
- 0-1 weakness badge
- Example: Hitman = `crowd_hyper`, `aggressive_performer`, `showman`

## MID TIER Battlers (3-4 badges)
- Mix of Rare positive and Common badges
- 1 weakness badge common
- Example: Chess = `scheme_specialist`, `structure_savant`, `underrated`

## LOW TIER Battlers (2-3 badges)
- Common badges
- 1-2 weakness badges
- Example: Footz = `street_battler`, `ring_rust`
