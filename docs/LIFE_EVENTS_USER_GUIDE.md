# Life Events & Storylines - Player Guide

Welcome to the Life Events system! This guide explains how real-life drama affects your battle rap career.

---

## Quick Overview

In Battle Rap University, your battler doesn't just exist on stage. They have a life - family, finances, legal issues, relationships, health, and street connections. These "life events" create storylines that:

- **Affect your stats** (reputation, stress, financial stability)
- **Cost prep days** (dealing with drama takes time)
- **Create lasting consequences** (a felony follows you forever)
- **Unlock or block opportunities** (can't book London with a felony)
- **Generate NPCs** (your baby mama, your OG, your manager)

---

## The Six Life Domains

Your battler's life is tracked across six categories:

### 1. Legal Status
| State | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Clean record | Full access to all leagues and events |
| Pending charges | Stress +10, some leagues hesitant |
| On probation | Can't travel, random drug tests |
| Felony conviction | No international bookings, permanent record |

### 2. Family Status
| State | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Supportive family | Family Bond provides stress buffer |
| Estranged | No family support, opponents have angles |
| Has children | Child support costs, custody storylines |
| Parent deceased | One-time grief event, permanent loss |

### 3. Financial Status
| State | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Stable | Can afford lawyers, managers, quality of life |
| In debt | Garnished earnings, stress from creditors |
| Tax issues | IRS problems, can't hide income |
| Bankruptcy | Rock bottom, rebuild from nothing |

### 4. Health Status
| State | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Healthy | Full performance |
| Injured | Reduced performance, may need to cancel |
| In rehab | Can't battle, focused on recovery |
| Chronic condition | Permanent modifier, manage symptoms |

### 5. Street Status
| State | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| No affiliation | Clean, no street angles against you |
| Gang affiliated | Crew loyalty matters, can't battle certain people |
| Has enemies | Risk of altercations, safety concerns |
| High heat | Active danger, may need to lay low |

### 6. Career Status
| State | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Independent | Keep all earnings, full control |
| Signed to label | Guaranteed money, less control |
| Has manager | Better bookings, costs percentage |
| Banned from league | Can't battle there anymore |

## How Storylines Work

### Triggering
Storylines start based on:
- **Random chance** after battles
- **Your attributes** (low financial stability = money problems)
- **Win/loss streaks** (3 losses = rivalry storylines)
- **Previous choices** (ignored family? They remember.)

### Chapters
Each storyline has 2-7 chapters. Each chapter:
1. Presents a situation
2. Gives you 2-3 choices
3. Shows consequences
4. Leads to next chapter or ending

### Urgency Levels
- **Passive** - Handle whenever
- **Timed** - Must choose within X hours or auto-resolves
- **Immediate** - Critical! Choose now.
- **Battle-gated** - Appears after your next battle

### Prep Day Costs
Dealing with life costs time. When a storyline costs prep days:
- Those days are removed from your prep calendar
- You get less practice before your next battle
- This is realistic - can't write bars while in court

## Example Storylines

### Family Drama (2-5 chapters)
*"Your mom called. Something's wrong at home."*

**Paths:**
- Go home immediately → Quick resolution, lose prep days
- Send money → Temporary fix, leads to more chapters
- Ignore it → Family estranged, long-term consequences

**Possible Endings:**
- Family First (positive) - Badge earned, family bond +1.5
- Estranged (negative) - Lost support system, stress +25

---

### Legal Troubles (3-6 chapters)
*"A process server showed up. Someone's suing you."*

**Paths:**
- Hire lawyer → Expensive but professional
- Ignore it → Default judgment, garnished wages
- Settle → Quick but costly

**Possible Endings:**
- Beat the Case (positive) - Rep +1.5, Badge: "Beat the Case"
- Did Time (catastrophic) - 30 days jail, cancelled battles

---

### Federal Heat (4-6 chapters)
*"Feds showed up at your door. They're not here about parking tickets."*

Based on real stories from battle rap (Tsu Surf, etc.)

**Paths:**
- Cooperate → Risk snitch label
- Lawyer up → Expensive legal battle
- Ignore → Arrest at venue, worse outcome

**Possible Endings:**
- Acquitted - Beat a RICO, legendary status
- Prison Time - 5 years, career on hold
- Free But Hated - Walked but called a snitch forever

---

### Punch Out (2-4 chapters)
*"They said something about your family. You swung on them. Cameras caught everything."*

Based on Math Hoffa incidents

**Paths:**
- Own it → Street respect, league consequences
- Apologize → Save career, lose street cred
- Deny → Evidence exists, looks worse

**Possible Endings:**
- Street Legend - Respected, but banned from leagues
- Reformed - Back in good standing, "soft" label
- Blackballed - No one will book you

## NPCs (Non-Player Characters)

Storylines can create permanent NPCs in your life:

| Type | How Created | Role |
|------|-------------|------|
| Girlfriend/Boyfriend | Romance storylines | Support or drama |
| Baby Mama/Daddy | Child storylines | Child support, custody |
| Manager | Career storylines | Better bookings, takes % |
| Lawyer | Legal storylines | Helps with cases |
| OG | Street storylines | Street guidance |
| Enemy | Various storylines | Source of future conflict |

NPCs have:
- **Name** (auto-generated or storyline-specific)
- **Relationship health** (1-10)
- **Status** (active, estranged, deceased)
- **History** (how you met, what happened)

## Strategic Tips

### Managing Storylines

1. **Check urgency** - Timed events auto-resolve badly if ignored
2. **Count prep days** - Don't trigger heavy storylines before big battles
3. **Consider long-term** - Short-term savings can create long-term problems
4. **Read endings** - Some paths lead to badges you might want

### Avoiding Bad Storylines

- Keep **financial stability** above 4 to avoid debt spirals
- Keep **family bond** above 5 to avoid estrangement
- Keep **stress** below 60 to avoid health issues
- Don't **ignore** timed events - consequences are worse

### Using Storylines Strategically

- Low rep? Take risks for potential reputation gains
- Need a badge? Some are only available through specific endings
- Want NPCs? Romance and career storylines create helpful characters

## FAQ

**Q: Can I avoid all storylines?**
A: No. Life happens. But keeping attributes balanced reduces negative triggers.

**Q: Do storylines affect battle simulation?**
A: Yes! Stress affects performance. Injuries can cancel battles. Legal issues block international events.

**Q: Can NPCs die?**
A: Yes. Some storylines can result in NPC death (family member passing, etc.)

**Q: How long do effects last?**
A: Permanent effects are forever. Temporary effects list their duration (days or battles).

**Q: What happens if I ignore a timed event?**
A: It auto-resolves, usually with the worst outcome. Don't ignore court dates.

| Term | Meaning |

| **Temporary Effect** | Stat change that expires |
