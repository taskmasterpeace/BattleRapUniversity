WHICH VIBE DO YOU WANT?

OPTION A: PIXEL/RETRO

  • ✓ Arcade/retro aesthetic
  • ✓ Nostalgic pixel art vibe
  • ✓ Unique, stands out
  • ✓ Pairs well with pixel sprites
  • ✗ Less readable at small sizes
  • ✗ Can feel gimmicky if overused
Best for:
Retro gaming aesthetic, pixel art emphasis, arcade nostalgia

OPTION B: CLEAN/MODERN

  • ✓ Professional esports look
  • ✓ Highly readable
  • ✓ Scales beautifully
  • ✓ Matches mockup aesthetic
  • ✗ Less unique/distinctive
  • ✗ More "standard" gaming UI
Best for:
Modern gaming UI, professional feel, maximum readability

📌 MY RECOMMENDATION

Looking at your mockups, they lean toward OPTION B (CLEAN/MODERN). But if you want that unique retro arcade personality, OPTION A (PIXEL) will make your game stand out more.

Good news: We can change this in literally 5 minutes after building the structure. Start with one, swap if it doesn't feel right!
